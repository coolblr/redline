## Scope & mode

Landing page. Mode: Persuade. Audience: the User named in PRD.md, about to accept a contract they cannot heavily negotiate.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Job: decide whether to sign. Action offered: try it on a document (upload). Proof: a real (synthetic-labeled) contract turning into a short ledger of flagged clauses, each citing its exact source sentence — no fabricated customers, prices, or quotes. Constraints: claims nothing PRD.md excludes — no verdict on signing, no legal advice, no scanned/photographed documents, no document types outside freelance/service agreements.
